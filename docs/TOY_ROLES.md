# Toy Roles

Terminal toys should not all sit on HOME as if they were equal room actions.

Miri should treat toys by role, so the room keeps its presence-first shape.

## Core Principle

A toy can be:

1. a room-presence toy,
2. an arcade toy,
3. a utility toy.

Those roles matter more than raw executable names.

## Room-Presence Toys

These strengthen the feeling that the deck is alive right now.

Best fits:
- `fortune`
- `cow`
- `clock`
- `garden` when it behaves well in the real tty target

Why they fit:
- fast to launch
- low cognitive load
- atmospheric or reflective
- easy to place inside Light Touch or Dwell

Layer fit:
- Light Touch: `fortune`, `cow`
- Dwell: `clock`, `garden`

Placement rules:
- may appear near first-screen prompts
- should be described as tiny rituals, not games menu items
- should support the room mood instead of distracting from it

Good copy:
- ask for a tiny omen
- let the room blink back
- keep a small clock lamp on
- sit in the garden for a minute

## Arcade Toys

These are playful side-room experiences.

Current examples:
- `train`
- `moon`
- `snake`
- `invaders`
- `tetris`
- `kitten`

Why they fit here:
- they are playful and fun
- they can be great reasons to visit
- but they should not dominate the first screen or primary room language

Placement rules:
- list them under `/toy` or a side-room surface
- do not let them compete with Bridge as the primary Deep Turn
- do not frame them as the soul of the product

Good copy:
- arcade side room
- small amusements
- drift for a minute
- play something tiny

## Utility Toys

These are shell-useful, but not strongly room-forward.

Current examples:
- `monitor`
- `files`

Why they belong apart:
- they may be practical
- but they read more like tooling than presence
- they weaken the illusion if they occupy too much first-screen attention

Placement rules:
- keep them under utilities, workshop, or maintenance language
- avoid foreground placement on HOME
- do not mix them with room-presence rituals in the same short list

## How `/toy` Should Read

`/toy` should feel like a side door, not the main hall.

Recommended grouping:

### Tiny room rituals
- `fortune`
- `cow`
- `clock`
- `garden`

### Arcade side room
- `train`
- `moon`
- `snake`
- `invaders`
- `tetris`
- `kitten`

### Utilities
- `monitor`
- `files`

## First-Screen Rule

HOME should privilege:
- one Light Touch action
- one Dwell action
- one Deep Turn action

Toys can support that, but should not replace it.

In practice:
- one room-presence toy may appear as a nearby suggestion
- arcade toys should stay one move away
- utility toys should stay in a secondary surface

## Cross-Doc Rule

When docs mention toys:
- avoid flat lists with no role distinction
- say whether a toy is presence, arcade, or utility
- keep Bridge as the primary Deep Turn
- keep Bridge Flash and doorbell stronger than generic toy launches on the first screen

## One-Sentence Summary

Toys are not one category; they are either room-presence rituals, arcade side rooms, or utility side tools.
